Our Legacy

A Piece of Corpus Christi History

Established in the mid-1950s, this historic building has served the community for generations. Formerly known as Super Max and Super Time Market, we are proud to continue the legacy as Prescott Meat Market.

Neighborhood Staple

Families have been shopping here since they were kids. We take pride in friendly service and high standards.

Quick In & Out

Skip the mega-store lines. We prioritize fast checkouts, easy parking, and immediately finding what you need.

Great Customer Service

We greet you with a smile. Whether it's a large catering order or a quick snack, our team goes above and beyond.
